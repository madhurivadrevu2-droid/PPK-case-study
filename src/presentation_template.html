Appendix

Definitions and method

MetricDefinition
VolumeProposals submitted, after removing duplicates and test records
ValueSum of proposal value in USD, after unit corrections
Win rateWon / (Won + Lost); Withdrawn (4.6% of closed) and open proposals excluded
TurnaroundBusiness days from submission to the first Won or Lost event, excluding weekends and public holidays
GrowthSame window each year: 1 Jan to 15 Oct for volume and value; cohorts submitted 1 Jan to 15 Jul for win rate and turnaround, so slow proposals have had time to close
Open / staleNo Won, Lost or Withdrawn event at the 15 Oct 2026 extract; stale = open more than 90 days
